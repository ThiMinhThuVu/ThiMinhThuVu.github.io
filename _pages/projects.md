# Projects

## TIGER Surgical AI Challenge 2026

<p class="status-label">Ongoing Research</p>

Research on semantic segmentation and surgical scene understanding for thoracoscopic and robot-assisted minimally invasive surgery. The project explores:

- Fine-grained surgical anatomy segmentation
- Cross-center generalization
- High-resolution segmentation
- Class imbalance
- Safety-aware segmentation error analysis
- Anatomical knowledge integration

## Temporal Scene Graphs for Surgical Video Understanding

<p class="status-label">Ongoing Research</p>

Reproduction and extension of scene-graph-based surgical video models on the Endoscapes dataset. This work studies temporal relationships among instruments, anatomical structures, and actions to support structured scene reasoning across video.

## Surgical VideoQA

<p class="status-label">Ongoing Research</p>

Development of multimodal surgical video question-answering pipelines. The current direction combines surgical-aware temporal reasoning, evidence retrieval, temporal grounding, and multimodal reasoning so that answers can be connected to supporting moments in a procedure.
